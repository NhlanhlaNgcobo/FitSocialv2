Privacy Policy

FitSocial · Last updated 19 August 2026

FitSocial is a fitness social app. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who else sees it, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read rather than to be survived, and it describes what the app actually does today.

The short version. Your account, your training history and your photos are stored in Google Firebase. Meal photos you choose to analyse are sent to an AI provider to be identified. Your location leaves the app in two cases: to fetch weather, and to draw the map of a run you recorded. We do not sell your data, and we do not run ads. You can delete everything from inside the app, permanently.

Who we are

FitSocial is built and operated from South Africa. For the purposes of the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), we are the responsible party for the personal information described here. Questions, requests and complaints go to info@miraistack.co.za.

Things collected automatically

  • Crash reports. When the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics receives a stack trace, your device model, OS version and app version, tagged with your account identifier — a random id, not your email or your name. This is how a crash gets fixed.
  • Usage analytics. Firebase Analytics records which screens are opened and which features are used, so we know what to build and what to remove.
  • Approximate location for weather. Your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to fetch the forecast shown in the app. Open-Meteo receives coordinates and nothing else — no account, no name, no device id.

Meal photos and AI

This one deserves saying plainly. When you photograph a meal and ask FitSocial to analyse it, the photo is uploaded to our storage and its address is passed to OpenRouter, which routes it to a vision model to identify the food and estimate its macros. The photo therefore leaves our systems and reaches a third-party AI provider.

Only photos you actively submit for analysis go through this. A meal you log by hand never does. Deleting the meal deletes the photo from our storage; it does not oblige a third-party provider to delete copies made under its own retention policy, and we cannot promise on their behalf.

Food names may also be looked up against the USDA FoodData Central database. That request carries the food name only.

What stays private

Some things are never visible to other users, no matter what:

  • Your email address and password
  • Your height, weight and BMI
  • Your GPS routes, unless you share the run
  • Your full food diary and its photos
  • Your unshared workouts and runs
  • Who you have muted or asked to be notified about

How long we keep it

Your content stays until you delete it or delete your account. Pulses expire on their own after 24 hours. Crash reports and analytics are retained on Firebase's own schedule, which is currently up to 90 days for crashes and up to 14 months for analytics.

Deleting your account

You can delete your FitSocial account, and everything attached to it, from inside the app:

  1. Open Profile → Settings
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account
  3. Type your username to confirm

This removes your profile, posts, Pulses, comments, reactions, follows, runs, workouts, meals, photos, challenge progress, points and badges. It is permanent and there is no undo — we cannot restore an account once it is gone.

Two things are deliberately not immediate. Your username is held for 14 days before anyone else can claim it, so that nobody can take the name of someone who has just left. And crash reports already sent are anonymous diagnostics that are not linked back to a deleted account; they age out on the schedule above.

If you cannot reach the app, email info@miraistack.co.za from your account's email address and we will delete it for you.

Your rights

Under POPIA — and equivalently under the GDPR if you are in Europe — you may ask us to confirm what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to how we use it. Most of this you can do yourself in the app: edit your profile, delete individual posts and logs, or delete the whole account. For anything else, write to info@miraistack.co.za and we will respond within 30 days. If you are unhappy with the outcome, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Children

FitSocial is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Access to your private data is enforced at the database by security rules, not only in the app — so a modified client cannot read another person's food diary or GPS routes. No system is perfect, and we will tell affected users and the Information Regulator if a breach ever puts personal information at risk.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who we share it with, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top is always the date of the current version.
