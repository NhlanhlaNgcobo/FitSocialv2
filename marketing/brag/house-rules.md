# FitSocial /brag house rules

Every /brag run for FitSocial reads this file first. These rules are what make
the output look like *our* app instead of a generic fitness ad.

## 1. Real screens only

- The app is Flutter (`fitsocial_app/lib/`). Screens are **rendered from the
  app's own widgets** by the shot harness in `fitsocial_app/tool/marketing_shots/`
  (1179x2556 PNGs, the app's dark theme, fake South African data). Run a shot
  with `tool/marketing_shots/run.sh <name>`; maps are laid in with
  `mapcomp.py` from OpenStreetMap streets in the app's map colours.
- Use those PNGs as the screens. Animate them (scroll the tall captures, flip
  through state captures such as the hold-to-alert frames), and draw only the
  status bar, phone frame and headline type in HTML.
- **Banned:** AI-generated phone mockups, stock athletes, 3D renders of
  phones on plinths, invented UI, invented stats, "streamline / unlock /
  elevate" SaaS copy. The old `marketing/2026-07-20/assets/fitsocial_carousel`
  and `fitsocial_uiux*` images are the look we are moving *away* from; do not
  reuse them.
- Only claim what the code actually does. If a claim is not backed by a file
  you read, cut it.

## 2. Brand

- Colours from `lib/app/theme/app_palette.dart` (dark theme):
  background `#050505`, surface `#111111`, surface-high `#1A1A1A`,
  stroke `#2B2B2B`, text `#F7F7F7`, muted `#AAAAAA`,
  brand orange `#FF6B1A`, brand-soft `#3C2113`, success `#31C46C`,
  danger `#E85D5D`.
- The app's UI face is **Roboto** (the platform default; the theme names no
  font). Headlines in the videos use **Anton** from
  `fitsocial_app/assets/fonts/` (the app's own "Strong" Pulse face) with
  Roboto for supporting lines.
- Logo/mark: `assets/images/fitsocial_f_mark.png`, `assets/icon/app_icon.png`.
- Tagline: **Train. Fuel. Share. Grow.**
- The app uses liquid-glass surfaces (`assets/shaders/liquid_glass.frag`,
  `glassTop/glassBottom/glassSheen` in the palette). Reproduce that look for
  sheets and the nav bar.

## 3. Content in the screens

- Sample data must feel South African and real: names like Lerato, Sipho,
  Thandi, Neo, Ayanda; places like Durban beachfront, Umhlanga, Cape Town
  Sea Point, Joburg; races like Comrades and Two Oceans; km and min/km;
  rands where money appears.
- Numbers must be plausible (a 5 km run at 5:30/km is 27:30, not 12:00).

## 4. Video (30 s clip)

- Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, 30 s.
- Beat sheet unless the prompt overrides it:
  0–3 s hook · 3–24 s three feature beats on real screens · 24–30 s end card
  (mark + "Train. Fuel. Share. Grow." + "FitSocial").
- On-screen text: max 6 words per line, readable in 1.5 s, inside the
  Instagram/TikTok safe zone (keep 250 px clear at the bottom, 200 px at top).
- Write `share-copy.txt` with an Instagram caption, a TikTok caption and
  5–8 hashtags (#FitSocial #RunSA #FitnessSouthAfrica …).

## 5. Carousel

/brag only renders a video by default. Every prompt also asks for a carousel,
so after the video:

- Build the slides from the **same app screens and kit** as the video
  (`carousel-src/`, generated by `tools/scaffold.py`), not a new design.
- 1080×1350 PNG (4:5), named `carousel_01.png` … into
  `<output-dir>/carousel/`.
- Slide 1 is a hook with a real screen in it; the last slide is a CTA.
  Slide numbers `01/0N` bottom-right in brand orange, as small Montserrat.
- One idea per slide. The screen sits in a simple rounded-rect phone frame
  (radius ~56 px, 10 px `#1A1A1A` bezel), never a photoreal phone.
- Write `carousel-copy.txt` with the caption for the post.
