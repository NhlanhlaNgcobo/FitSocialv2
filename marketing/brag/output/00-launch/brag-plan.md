# Brag Plan: FitSocial (launch clip)

## What is this app?
A South African fitness social app: you track runs and workouts, log meals from a photo, post it all to a feed and Pulses, race friends in challenges, and can send a silent alert to your safety contacts.

## The angle
"Your whole fitness life, one feed." Not a feature list: the video is a phone in your hand, moving through the real app the way a user's day moves. Feed, run, meal, Pulse, challenge, safety. Every screen is the app's own widgets rendered with fictional South African data (Durban beachfront route on real OpenStreetMap streets, a braai plate, Comrades training).

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
Anton headline slams in on the app's black: "YOUR WHOLE FITNESS LIFE." then "ONE FEED." in brand orange, while the phone rises into frame with the Home feed already alive (Pulse rings, 5-day streak).

## Key moments
- Feed scrolls past Sipho's 11.8 km beachfront run card and Thandi's leg-day log.
- Live Run: map with the route on Durban streets, 4.21 km / 23:05 / 5:29.
- Meal Review: "Braai plate", 1029 kcal, four detected foods matched to the database.
- Pulse viewer ("Comrades in 8 weeks. No days off.") then the Road to Comrades leaderboard.
- Safety: the SOS button fills as it is held, then "Alerting 3 contacts".

## Outro / punchline
Phone drops away; the FitSocial wordmark lands on a bell: "Train. Fuel. Share. Grow."

## User flow worth showing
Open the app to your crew's feed → track a run live → log the meal after → post a Pulse and check the challenge → and if it goes wrong on the road, hold one button.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: clean feature film, real screens, one idea per beat
- Interpretation: slide transitions inside the phone like app navigation, steady 5 s beats, headline + one supporting line, no jokes.

## Format: vertical, 1080x1920
## Duration: 30 s (set by the house rules)

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #050505 with the app's warm ember ground (#3C2113 brand-soft)
- Accent: #FF6B1A
- Text: #F7F7F7, muted #AAAAAA
- Display font: Anton (the app's "Strong" Pulse face)
- Body font: Roboto (the app's UI face)
- Strongest visual element: the orange route and the glass cards on black

## Share copy (draft)
FitSocial is live: track the run, log the braai, post the Pulse, race your crew, and hold one button if you ever need help.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with a consistent light SFX layer
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (120 BPM), 0.34, fade out over the last 1.5 s
- Music cue guidance: preset read. Scene changes on the beat grid: 3.02, 8.02, 13.01, 18.02 (strong), 20.52, 23.02 (strong), 27.02. Headline lines hold at least 1.5 s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; bass swells the ember glow behind the phone. No visualiser graphics.
- SFX posture: light, 0.6-0.75 volume. Soft drop on each screen change, a click when the thumb lands on SOS, a soft impact when the alert sends, bell on the wordmark.
- Restraint rule: nothing on every beat; no SFX on text-only changes.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 0.0-3.0 s
"YOUR WHOLE FITNESS LIFE." (two lines) then "ONE FEED." in orange. Phone rises from below, Home feed on screen.
Sequential/interaction: the two headline parts arrive one after the other (0.2 s, 1.1 s).
Audio: music in; soft impact as the phone lands.
Transition: slide → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Feed — 3.0-8.0 s
Headline "RUNS. LIFTS. MEALS." / "From the people you train with." Feed scrolls down to the run card and on to the workout card; glass top bar and floating nav stay put.
Sequential/interaction: continuous scroll, eases to a stop on the workout card.
Transition: push → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Train — 8.0-13.0 s
"TRACK IT LIVE." / "Route, pace and time while you run." Live Run slides in like a pushed route; slow punch-in on the map card.
Transition: push → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Fuel — 13.0-18.0 s
"SNAP THE PLATE." / "Every food itemised. Macros added up." Meal Review slides in, scrolls to the detected foods.
Transition: push → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Share — 18.0-23.0 s
18.0-20.5 "POST THE MOMENT." / "Pulses stay up for 24 hours." Pulse viewer; the progress bar keeps running.
20.5-23.0 "CALL OUT YOUR CREW." / "Set a goal. Race your friends to it." Challenge board scrolls to the leaderboard.
Transition: push → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Safe — 23.0-27.0 s
"HOLD FOR 2 SECONDS." / "A silent alert with your live location." Safety screen; a touch dot lands on SOS and the fill sweeps across (six captured frames), then "Alerting 3 contacts".
Sequential/interaction: simulated press and hold.
Transition: phone falls away → Scene 7

### Scene 7 — End card — 27.0-30.0 s
F mark + "itSocial" wordmark (as the app's FitSocialLogo builds it), then "Train. Fuel. Share. Grow."
Audio: bell on the wordmark; music fades.

**Music mood:** upbeat, clean
**Audio summary:** a warm bed that carries six clean screen changes and lands on a single bell.
