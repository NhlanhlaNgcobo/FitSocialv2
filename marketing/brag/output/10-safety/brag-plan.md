## Storyboard
- 0.15-3.2 s: Run free. Someone's got you.
- 3.27-8.74 s: Pick your people. / Up to three: FitSocial friends or email.
- 8.74-13.11 s: Hold for 2 seconds. / A silent alert. Nothing sounds on your phone.
- 13.11-17.47 s: No time to unlock? / Send from the app icon, mid-run, or the volume buttons.
- 17.47-21.2 s: They see where you are. / Your live location, until you enter your safe PIN.
- 21.2-25.3 s: Forced to cancel? / A duress PIN looks like it ends the alert. Your contacts are told.
- End: F mark + itSocial wordmark, "Train. Fuel. Share. Grow."
