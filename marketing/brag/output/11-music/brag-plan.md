- 0.15-3.1 s: Your soundtrack, in the app.

- 8.44-14.22 s: Control it mid-run. / Skip and pause without leaving your run.
- 14.22-19.48 s: Tap the note. / Your music drops down from the top of Home.
- 19.48-24.7 s: Share the song. / Put what you're playing on your Pulse.
- End: F mark + itSocial wordmark, "Train. Fuel. Share. Grow."
